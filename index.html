One-Time Phone Setup (60 Seconds)

Zero App Install
  1. On your Android phone, open Settings → About Phone and tap Build Number 7 times.
  2. Open Settings → Developer Options and enable USB Debugging (and USB Debugging Security Settings on Xiaomi/Poco/Vivo).
  3. Connect your phone to your PC/Laptop/Tablet and click Pair Android via WebUSB above, then tap Allow on your phone once.
  4. Return to PC Desktop — every mouse click, drag swipe, and keyboard key now operates your real phone screen even when Chrome on your phone is closed and the phone display is turned OFF!
